CroxyProxy does not host the websites that are opened through it. If you believe content accessible through this service infringes your copyright, email a notice to contact@croxyproxycom.com that includes:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • The copyrighted work and the address of the material.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized.
  • A statement that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.
