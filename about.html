CroxyProxy is a free web proxy. You enter a website address, and the proxy opens that page for you and sends it back to your browser. The site you visit sees the proxy’s address instead of yours.

What it is good for

  • Opening pages that are blocked on a school, office or public network.
  • Quick access to a single site without installing an app.
  • Checking how a page looks from another location.

What it is not

A web proxy is not a VPN. It only handles the page you open through it, and it does not make you fully anonymous. Please do not enter banking details or other highly sensitive passwords.

Our aim

Keep the tool simple, free and clear about its limits.
