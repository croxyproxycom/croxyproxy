Frequently asked questions

Is this web proxy free?

Yes. There is no account and nothing to download.

Is a web proxy the same as a VPN?

No. A web proxy only handles the page you open through it. A VPN handles all traffic from your device.

Can I log in to websites through the proxy?

You can, but we advise against entering banking or other highly sensitive passwords in any web proxy.

Why does a site not load properly?

Some sites use heavy scripts or block proxies. Try again, clear your cache, or try a different link.

Do you store my browsing history?

We do not ask you to create an account. Our privacy policy explains exactly what is logged.
