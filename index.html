An online proxy for everyday browsing

A web proxy sits between your browser and the website you want to open. You enter an address, the proxy requests the page on your behalf, and then it sends the result back to you. The website sees the proxy’s address instead of yours, and you do not need an app, an extension or a VPN client.

It is a simple tool for a simple job: reaching a page that is slow, restricted on your current network, or that you would rather not open directly from your own connection. CroxyProxy is free and works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.

Key advantages

  • Free to use, with no account and no download.
  • Works in Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge on Windows, Android, iPhone, Mac and Chromebook.
  • Only the page you open goes through the proxy. The rest of your traffic stays direct.
  • Opens older HTTP pages through an encrypted connection to us.
  • Dedicated pages with tips for the sites people open most.

How this free proxy works

  1. Enter the address

    Type or paste a link, or a search phrase.

  2. Start the proxy

    The proxy fetches the page. It usually takes a few seconds.

  3. Browse

    Links inside the page keep opening through the proxy.

Behind the scenes, the proxy server downloads the page and rewrites its links, so images, scripts and every further click also pass through the proxy. That is why you can keep browsing normally. It is also why very heavy pages can load more slowly than on a direct connection, because each request makes an extra stop.

Tips for the best results

  • Paste the full link of the page, including https://.
  • Open one page at a time instead of many tabs, so the server is not overloaded.
  • Reload once if a page looks broken, and clear your browser cache if it keeps happening.
  • Choose lower video quality on a slow connection.
  • If a site refuses to load, it may block proxies. Try another page or try again later.

Staying safe when you use a proxy

A web proxy is useful, but it is not a magic shield. Keep these habits:

  • Do not enter banking details or other highly sensitive passwords through any web proxy.
  • Log out of accounts when you finish on a shared device.
  • Keep your browser up to date.
  • Follow the terms of the sites you visit and the rules of your network.

A web proxy only covers the page you open through it. For protection of all your device traffic, a VPN is the better tool.

Common problems and fixes

  • Blank page: reload, then try the direct link of an inner page.
  • Missing images: large images can time out, so reload the page.
  • Video will not play: lower the quality or try again when the server is less busy.
  • Login fails: sites often block logins from proxies. We advise against logging in anyway.
