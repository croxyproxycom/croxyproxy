## About this page

Facebook pages depend on logins and many scripts. Public pages are the most likely to open, while features tied to your account can fail through a proxy.

## Tips for better results

- Public pages and posts are the best candidates.
- Do not enter your Facebook password on a web proxy.
- If the page is blank, reload it or try the mobile version of the link.

A web proxy does not change the rules of Facebook or your network. Follow their terms and your local laws.
