People often use the words web proxy and VPN as if they mean the same thing. They do not, and picking the wrong one can leave you with less privacy than you expected.

## What a web proxy does

A web proxy opens one page for you and passes it back to your browser. The website sees the proxy’s address instead of yours. Only the page you open through the proxy is affected. The rest of your device keeps using its normal connection.

## What a VPN does

A VPN creates an encrypted tunnel for all traffic from your device or from selected apps. It covers your browser, apps and background services. It usually needs an app and often costs money.

## Which one fits your case

- **Quick access to a single page:** a web proxy is enough and needs no setup.
- **Privacy on public Wi-Fi:** a VPN covers more of what you do.
- **Everyday use for many apps:** choose a VPN.
