## About this page

TikTok is built around video, so playback speed matters most. Direct video links usually behave better than the home feed.

## Tips for better results

- Paste the link of a single video rather than the home feed.
- Expect slower loading than a direct connection.
- Reload if the video does not start.

A web proxy does not change the rules of TikTok or your network. Follow their terms and your local laws.
