## About this page

YouTube pages load a lot of scripts and streaming data, so a proxy is slower than a direct connection. These tips help you get a smoother result.

## Tips for better results

- Paste the full video link, not just the site name.
- Choose a lower video quality if playback keeps stopping.
- Refresh the page or try again later if the server is busy.
- Avoid signing in to your Google account on any web proxy.

A web proxy does not change the rules of YouTube or your network. Follow their terms and your local laws.
