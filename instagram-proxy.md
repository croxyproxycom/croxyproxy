## About this page

Public profiles and posts are the pages most likely to load. Features that need an account may not work, and Instagram may treat unusual logins as suspicious.

## Tips for better results

- Public profiles and posts load most reliably.
- Logging in through a proxy can trigger security checks, so we advise against it.
- Clear your browser cache if a page looks broken.

A web proxy does not change the rules of Instagram or your network. Follow their terms and your local laws.
